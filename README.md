### AI-Powered Smart Waste Segregation & Sustainability Assistant

AI WasteWise is a web-based sustainability solution that helps users identify different types of waste and understand how to dispose of them correctly.

The project combines **Artificial Intelligence and sustainability** to make waste segregation simpler, faster, and more accessible.

---

## 🌱 Problem

Incorrect waste segregation is a common problem.

People often do not know whether an item belongs to:

- Wet waste
- Dry waste
- Recyclable waste
- E-waste
- Hazardous waste

This can lead to recyclable materials being wasted, harmful materials being disposed of incorrectly, and increased environmental pollution.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**AI WasteWise** provides a simple interface where users can enter a waste item or upload an image.

The system is designed to:

1. Accept a waste item through text or image.
2. Analyze the waste using AI.
3. Identify its waste category.
4. Provide suitable disposal guidance.
5. Give a sustainability tip to encourage better environmental practices.

### Example

**Input:** `Plastic Bottle`

**AI Classification:** `Recyclable / Dry Waste`

**Guidance:** Clean and empty the bottle, then place it in the appropriate recyclable/dry-waste collection stream.

**Sustainability Tip:** Reuse a bottle where practical or choose reusable alternatives.
